# Refund policy

Northwind Cloud customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of a charge. Annual plans are refundable pro rata during the first 30 days; after that, annual plans are not refundable.

## Approval rules

Support agents can approve refunds up to 200 USD on their own. Refunds between 200 and 2,000 USD need approval from a support team lead. Refunds above 2,000 USD need approval from the finance director and a ticket reference.

## Duplicate charges

Duplicate charges caused by a billing error are always refunded in full, regardless of plan or amount, and do not need team lead approval. The refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Credits instead of refunds

Customers can choose service credits instead of a refund. Credits are worth 120% of the refunded amount and expire after 12 months.
