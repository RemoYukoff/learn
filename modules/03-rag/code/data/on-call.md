# On-call rotation

Engineers join the on-call rotation after three months at the company. Each rotation lasts one week, from Monday 10:00 to the next Monday 10:00 (UTC-3).

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a fixed stipend of 300 USD per week. Every page that happens between 22:00 and 07:00 gives one extra day off, up to three days per rotation.

## Handover

The outgoing engineer writes a handover note listing open incidents, noisy alerts and pending follow-ups. The handover happens in the #oncall channel before 10:00 on Monday.

## Escalation

If the primary on-call does not acknowledge a page within the severity deadline, the page escalates to the secondary on-call, and after another 10 minutes to the engineering manager.
