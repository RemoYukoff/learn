# Plans and pricing

| Plan | Price | Seats included | Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 29 USD per month | 3 | Email, next business day |
| Business | 249 USD per month | 25 | Email and chat, 4 business hours |
| Enterprise | Custom, from 2,500 USD per month | Unlimited | 24/7 phone, 1 hour for SEV1 |

Extra seats cost 12 USD per seat per month on Starter and 9 USD on Business. Annual billing gives a 15% discount on Starter and Business.

Single sign-on (SAML and OIDC) is only available on the Enterprise plan. Audit logs are available on Business and Enterprise.
