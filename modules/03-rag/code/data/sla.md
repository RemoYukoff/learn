# Service level agreement (SLA)

Northwind Cloud guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime on the Starter plan, 99.95% on the Business plan and 99.99% on the Enterprise plan.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the guarantee, customers receive service credits: 10% of the monthly fee for each 0.1 percentage point below the target, capped at 50% of the monthly fee. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident through a support ticket.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance, outages caused by the customer's own configuration, and force majeure events do not count as downtime.

## Measurement

Uptime is measured every minute by external probes in three regions. A minute counts as down when two of the three regions fail.
