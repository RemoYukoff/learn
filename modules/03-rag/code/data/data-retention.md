# Data retention

Customer application logs are kept for 30 days on Starter, 90 days on Business and 400 days on Enterprise.

## Backups

Databases are backed up every 6 hours. Backups are encrypted and kept for 35 days in a second region.

## Account deletion

When a customer closes an account, their data is deleted within 30 days. Backups that contain the data expire naturally within the following 35 days. Customers can request an export in JSON or CSV before closing the account.

## Legal holds

Data under a legal hold is excluded from automatic deletion until the legal team releases the hold.
