# Support channels and hours

Support is available by email on all plans, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 (UTC-3). Business customers also get live chat in the same hours. Enterprise customers get a 24/7 phone line.

## First response targets

Starter tickets get a first response by the next business day. Business tickets get a first response within 4 business hours. Enterprise SEV1 tickets get a first response within 1 hour, any time of day.

## Languages

Support is provided in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

## Holidays

Email and chat support are closed on January 1 and December 25. The Enterprise phone line stays open every day of the year.
