# Expense policy

Employees can expense work equipment up to 1,500 USD per year without prior approval. Anything above that needs manager approval before the purchase.

## Travel

Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy. Hotels are reimbursed up to 180 USD per night in most cities and up to 260 USD per night in New York, London and San Francisco.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,000 USD per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted with a receipt within 60 days. Expenses submitted later are not reimbursed.
